A Michelin-star food trip to La Manchuela, Spain

Away from the coast, 1h30 inland from Valencia, oba- is in Casas-Ibáñez, a quiet corner of Spain between the Júcar and Cabriel river gorges. Come for a Michelin-starred lunch, explore the cave villages along the river and, if you like, stay the night in the same building.

1h30

From Valencia

By car on the A-3 and N-322. After Requena the road drops down to the Cabriel river and into La Manchuela.

An easy day trip: set off in the morning, walk by the river at La Terrera, have lunch at oba- and drive back in the afternoon. Or skip the drive home with our Night Pack.

DIRECTIONS FROM VALENCIA

2h

From Alicante and the Costa Blanca

On the A-31 towards Almansa, then country roads through La Manchuela.

On the way, stop at Alcalá del Júcar, a village built into the river canyon, 15 minutes from the restaurant. To avoid driving back at night, dinner with a hotel room is the most relaxed option.

DIRECTIONS FROM ALICANTE

2h

From Murcia

On the A-30, via Albacete.

Just before you arrive, Jorquera looks out over a bend in the Júcar. Best as an overnight stay: dinner, a room in the same building and the next morning for the river villages.

DIRECTIONS FROM MURCIA

35 min

From Albacete

On the N-322.

Close enough for lunch or dinner and home again. For a weekend nearby, the Ruta del Molinar trail and the Cueva de los Ángeles cave are just around the corner.

DIRECTIONS FROM ALBACETE

2h30

From Madrid

On the A-3, the Madrid–Valencia motorway.

Best with a night at the hotel: arrive in the afternoon, have the Cuaderno Medio dinner at oba-, sleep at Cañitas Maite Gastronómico in the same building and see Alcalá del Júcar the next day before heading back.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
CAN I DO IT AS A DAY TRIP?
Yes, from Albacete or Valencia it's easy. From Alicante, Murcia or Madrid we recommend staying the night: the hotel is in the same building as the restaurant.
WHEN ARE YOU OPEN?
Thursday to Sunday, lunch (1pm–5pm) and dinner (8pm–11:30pm).
WHERE CAN I STAY?
At Hotel Cañitas Maite Gastronómico, in the same building. The Night Pack includes the Cuaderno Medio dinner at oba- and a hotel night with breakfast for €215 per person.
HOW DO I GET THERE FROM ABROAD?
Fly into Valencia (1h30 by car) or Alicante (2h). By high-speed train from Madrid, get off at Requena-Utiel (Valencia line) or Albacete (35 minutes away) and continue by car or taxi. A car is the easiest way to explore La Manchuela.
WHAT IS THERE TO SEE NEARBY?
Alcalá del Júcar is 15 minutes away, with Jorquera, the Cueva de los Ángeles cave and the Cabriel gorges close by.
IS THERE PARKING?
Yes, free parking right across the street.
